CANDIDATES
==========

[1] harbor — boats tied for the night (FRONT-RUNNER)
The boats are tied for the night,
each nodding at its own rope.
The harbor keeps them the way a hand keeps a coin:
loosely, enough to rise and fall.
The water takes the last light and spreads it thin.
No one is owed anything until morning.
~237 code points. One unfolding image (harbor at dusk); "kept loosely"
carries "held" without saying it; final line releases all obligations —
nothing to prove. Open vowels: boats, rope, coin, loosely, water.

[2] dog — sleeping in the last patch of sun
The dog sleeps in the patch of sun by the door,
paws slack, dreaming of somewhere far to smell.
The hose drips into the bucket: one note, then the same note.
Nothing here is late.
Even the fence's shadow, reaching him, lies down.
~223 code points. Warm, domestic; the shadow lying down is a fresh turn.
Slightly closer to stock "sleeping pet" territory.

[3] bread — loaf cooling on the rack
The loaf came out of the oven an hour ago
and is still deciding to be bread.
Its crust ticks as it cools,
like a house settling into its own skin.
Nobody needs it yet.
It gives the last of its heat to the room,
and the room, without being asked, is warmer.
~250 code points. "Still deciding to be bread" is fresh; ending leans
a touch aphoristic.

[4] rain — storm easing at evening
Rain on the roof, but less now —
the fat drops only, spaced out like slow clocks.
Under the eave a sparrow waits, dry already,
feathers back in order.
The sky is the color of a towel wrung out.
The wet street shines the last light back up,
and the houses sit in it, done.
~260 code points. "Towel wrung out" sky is good; two images (sparrow,
street) dilute the single-image focus.

[5] dusk — light climbing the west wall
Evening comes in without knocking
and lies along the west wall,
climbing a hand's width each minute.
The dust in the light doesn't hurry.
Across the street, one window holds its square of gold,
then gives it back.
The room goes blue.
Nothing ends. It only gets softer.
~265 code points. Gentle but a little mannered; "without knocking"
is near-stock.

DECISION: [1] harbor. Strongest: freshest central image (harbor keeps
them the way a hand keeps a coin — loosely), single place held for the
whole span, short declarative beats between long open lines, and the
last line ("No one is owed anything until morning") keeps working after
the file ends — relief shown, never instructed.
